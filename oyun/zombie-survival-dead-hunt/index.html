Özel Tuş ve Kontrol Bilgisi: Move through the danger zone and fight approaching zombies. Use WASD to move, mouse to aim, and Left Mouse Button to shoot. Press R to reload and Space to jump. Your goal is simple: kill zombies, stay alive, and achieve the highest survival score!
