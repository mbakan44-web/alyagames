📖 Panda Restaurant Hakkında

Welcome to Panda Restaurant and build the cutest food empire in the forest! In this exciting restaurant adventure, you play as an adorable panda trying to survive in a world where endangered animals no longer receive special treatment. Run your own restaurant and earn more income! It is a fun mix of simulation gameplay and addictive time management game mechanics. The cute art style, satisfying progression, and exploration features make it one of the most enjoyable animal games online.
