📖 Apocalypse Shelter Hakkında

The world has fallen — but survival is still possible. Build and expand your shelter, manage resources, and create a safe haven in a hostile environment. Assemble a squad of unique heroes, each with their own abilities and combat style. Engage in tactical turn-based battles, plan your moves, and use your team’s skills to overcome dangerous enemies. Unlock new characters from chests, upgrade their cards, and build the ultimate squad to survive the apocalypse.

🕹️ Nasıl Oynanır ve Kontroller

This is a mix of strategy, management, and tactical combat set in a post-apocalyptic world. You control a shelter: build rooms, manage resources, and make decisions that affect survival. But staying inside is not enough. Expeditions into the wasteland are essential — and dangerous. Every battle requires planning and smart decision-making. Each character plays a role in your strategy. Some deal damage, others heal or control the battlefield. Only the right combination will lead you to victory.
