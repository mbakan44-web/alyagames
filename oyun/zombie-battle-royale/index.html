📖 Zombie Battle Royale Hakkında

Get ready for a zombie io style battle royale! In this shooter, you'll have to survive in the arena against dozens of opponents. Search for powerful weapons, gather rare loot, and remember: the safe zone is shrinking rapidly. Become the last survivor in this high-octane action survival game!

🕹️ Nasıl Oynanır ve Kontroller

Controls On Mobile Devices (Touchscreen): Left Joystick — move character. Firearm button — shoot. Melee weapon button — strike. Grenade button — throw a grenade at the enemy. Energy drink button — quick dash (lunge) forward. On PC (Keyboard & Mouse): WASD — move character. LMB (Left Mouse Button) — fire weapon. RMB (Right Mouse Button) — melee attack. [ E ] Key — throw a grenade. [ R ] Key — use energy drink (quick dash).
